        Auto-play

        Picks the source by itself when you play. The regex is matched against each source's addon, name, title, description and URL (same as Nuvio TV).

        Custom poster

        URL pattern from a poster service. Placeholders: {id}, {imdb_id}, {tmdb_id}, {tvdb_id}, {kitsu_id}, {type}, {id_type}, {typed_id}. Add ? to make it optional: {imdb_id?}.

        Nuvio account

        You can also sign in with QR from the Roku: Settings > Account > Sign in with QR.

        Your own server (optional)

        The channel uses the official Nuvio server. Only change this if you run your own Nuvio server (self-host).

        TMDB

        Metadata, logos and episodes in your language. Optional: if the channel already includes a key you do not need yours. You can also use your own free key from themoviedb.org.

        MDBList

        Ratings from IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic, Letterboxd and more. Free key at mdblist.com.

        Trakt and Simkl

        No apps or keys needed. Connect your account from the Roku in Settings > Tracking and scan the code.
